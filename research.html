Ongoing Projects

Q-POPs: Systematic Monitoring of Qatar’s Persistent Organic Pollutants

This project focuses on systematic monitoring of Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs) in Qatar to comply with the Stockholm Convention and Qatar National Vision 2030 environmental stewardship targets. It includes the development of a private blockchain platform using Hyperledger to establish the Qatar Comprehensive Database for Environmental Contaminants (Q-CDEC).

The blockchain-based system securely stores and processes POPs data from soil, water, sediments, and vegetation using advanced analytical techniques such as GC/MS. It ensures transparency, immutability, and tamper-proof data storage to support long-term environmental management, research, and policy making.

Blockchain-based Authentication for Decentralized Finance (DeFi)

As Primary Investigator with Hail University, KSA, Dr. Uddin is working on blockchain-based authentication mechanisms to enhance security and privacy in DeFi. The research explores blockchain tokenization and NFTs to establish secure digital identities for stakeholders and addresses vulnerabilities in traditional authentication.

The project proposes a four-phase model covering identity digitization, NFT-based authentication, and deployment on private and public blockchains, aiming to improve confidentiality, integrity, and trust in financial transactions while addressing scalability and regulatory challenges.

Post-Quantum Resistant Cryptographic Systems for Critical National Infrastructure

Dr. Uddin is involved in research on secure, post-quantum-resistant cryptographic systems to protect critical national infrastructure, including blockchain-based, decentralized, private PKI for secure communication between CNI assets.

The work integrates quantum-resistant algorithms into permissioned blockchain environments and aims to develop a secure, private digital Certificate Authority reinforced with post-quantum cryptographic protocols.

Blockchain Architectures for Healthcare and Smart Cities

Previous work includes blockchain-enabled healthcare projects funded by the Ministry of Health, UAE, at Khalifa University, involving Ethereum smart contracts, Hyperledger Fabric, Hyperledger Besu, and Quorum for medical and healthcare supply chain applications.

Additional research includes blockchain architectures for NFT-based secure asset authentication in cyber-physical systems and the Decentralized Smart City of Things (DSCoT) architecture, extending NFT protocols for secure IoT asset digitization and authentication in smart city environments.
