Biography

Dr. Mueen Uddin is a distinguished Senior Member of IEEE and a Professional Member of ACM and AIS, with over 15 years of professional experience in research, teaching, and development. Recognized globally for his scholarly contributions, Stanford University and Elsevier listed him among the Top 2% of Research Scientists from 2022 to 2024. Dr. Mueen earned his PhD from Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) in 2013, following his MS and BS in Computer Science in 2008 and 2005, respectively. He is currently an Associate Professor of Data & Cybersecurity at the University of Doha for Science and Technology in Qatar. Dr. Mueen has established a strong track record of research and academic leadership, successfully acquiring, managing, and implementing numerous research-funded projects in collaboration with agencies across Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Brunei Darussalam.

His prolific publication record includes 200 peer-reviewed publications in high-impact journals and conferences. Dr. Mueen serves in various editorial roles, including Editor, Associate Editor, and Guest Editor, for prestigious journals and conferences. His research expertise centers on the security of large-scale distributed systems, encompassing Cloud, Edge, IoT, IoE, IIoT, and Cyber-Physical Systems, as well as the Security of Healthcare Supply Chain Systems, Healthcare Informatics, and emerging technologies such as Blockchain, NFTs, the Metaverse, and Web3.0. Dr. Mueen's contributions continue to drive innovation in data and cybersecurity, underscoring his commitment to advancing knowledge and shaping the future of digital security.

Current Position & Interests

Dr. Uddin’s work focuses on cybersecurity, blockchain and distributed ledger technologies, cloud computing, digital forensics, and energy-efficient computing for data centers and smart environments. His ongoing projects address environmental stewardship, DeFi security, post-quantum cryptography for critical infrastructure, and blockchain applications in healthcare and smart cities.
