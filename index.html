Short Biography

Currently, Dr. Mueen Uddin is an Associate Professor of Data and Cybersecurity at the College of Computing and Information Technology, University of Doha for Science & Technology, Doha, Qatar. His primary motivations for employment are teaching, research, and community service, and he maintains a balance by demonstrating strong competency in Research (60%), Teaching (30%), and Community Service (10%).

He completed his PhD in Information Systems at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) in 2013. His doctoral thesis, titled “Energy Efficient and Low Carbon Enabler Green IT Framework for Data Centers in Pakistan,” proposed an implementation framework to evaluate and deploy energy-efficient cloud data centers, with key findings published in the Tier-1 journal Renewable & Sustainable Energy Reviews.

Throughout his academic career, he has gained international recognition through innovative research, strong leadership, and hands-on teaching in Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics, Business Information Systems, Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology, Information & Network Security, and Cloud Computing. He has held academic positions and conducted research in Malaysia, UAE, KSA, Pakistan, Brunei, and Qatar.

Current Position & Interests

Dr. Uddin’s work focuses on cybersecurity, blockchain and distributed ledger technologies, cloud computing, digital forensics, and energy-efficient computing for data centers and smart environments. His ongoing projects address environmental stewardship, DeFi security, post-quantum cryptography for critical infrastructure, and blockchain applications in healthcare and smart cities.
