📖 Biography

Dr. Mueen Uddin is a distinguished Senior Member of IEEE and a Professional Member of ACM and AIS, with over 15 years of professional experience in research, teaching, and development. Recognized globally for his scholarly contributions, Stanford University and Elsevier listed him among the Top 2% of Research Scientists from 2022 to 2024. Dr. Mueen earned his PhD from Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) in 2013, following his MS and BS in Computer Science in 2008 and 2005, respectively.

His prolific publication record includes 200 peer-reviewed publications in high-impact journals and conferences. His research expertise centers on the security of large-scale distributed systems, encompassing Cloud, Edge, IoT, IoE, IIoT, and Cyber-Physical Systems, as well as emerging technologies such as Blockchain, NFTs, the Metaverse, and Web3.0.
