About Me

I am currently an Associate Professor of Data and Cybersecurity at the College of Computing and Information Technology, University of Doha for Science & Technology, Qatar. My professional journey is defined by a balance of rigorous research (60%), hands-on teaching (30%), and impactful community service (10%).

I completed my PhD in Information Systems at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) in 2013, focusing on "Energy Efficient and Low Carbon Enabler Green IT Framework for Data Centers." Throughout my career, I have gained international recognition for innovative research in Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics, Blockchain, and Cloud Computing.

I am honored to be listed among the top 2% of research scientists globally by Stanford University and Elsevier for consecutive years (2022-2025). My work aims to bridge the gap between theoretical frameworks and real-world applications, particularly in securing critical national infrastructures and developing sustainable computing solutions.
