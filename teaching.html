Teaching Overview

Teaching is a core component of Dr. Uddin’s academic profile, forming approximately 30% of his professional activities alongside 60% research and 10% community service. His teaching experience spans multiple countries and institutions, covering a wide range of courses in computer science, information systems, cybersecurity, and networks.

He has consistently achieved faculty performance evaluation scores above 90% over the past eight years at universities in Malaysia, UAE, KSA, Pakistan, and Brunei, reflecting his commitment to high-quality, student-centered teaching.
