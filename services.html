Professional and Academic Service

Dr. Uddin’s professional responsibilities include significant service to the academic and research communities, complementing his activities in teaching and research. His service portfolio spans standards development, editorial work, conference leadership, and technical program committees.

Standards and Working Groups

  • Working Group Member, IEEE Standards Association (SA) – EDU P3722 Guide for Responsible Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Higher Education, contributing to ethical and responsible AI integration in higher education settings.

Editorial and Review Activities

Dr. Uddin has engaged in extensive editorial, review, and academic service across various journals and conferences, reflecting his leadership and expertise in cybersecurity, cloud computing, and blockchain technologies.

Conference Leadership and Committees

  • Scientific Committee Chair, 16th Learning & Technology Conference 2019 (Elsevier indexed), Effat University, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.
  • Track Chair, ICT for Sustainability, International Conference on Information and Communication Technology for the Muslim World (ICT4M) 2013, Rabat, Morocco.
  • Track Chair, e-Service / Ad hoc and Sensor Networks / Internet and Web Applications, World Congress on Computing and Information Technology (WCIT) 2013, Asia Pacific University of Technology and Innovation (APU), Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
  • Track Chair, Wireless Communication and Data Mining, WCIT 2014, Asia Pacific University of Technology and Innovation, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
  • Program Committee Member, 5th International Conference on Higher Education Advances (HEAd’19), Valencia, Spain.
  • Scientific Advisory Board Member, 4th Renewable Energy Sources – Research and Business Conference (RESRB), Wrocław, Poland.
  • Technical Program Committee member and session chair at multiple international conferences including BRAINS 2021 and BRAINS 2022, and the 4th Blockchain and Internet of Things Conference (BIOTC 2022).

Community and Institutional Service

In addition to global academic service, Dr. Uddin provides institutional and community service in roles such as curriculum development, research group leadership, supervision of postgraduate students, and participation in research grants aligned with national and regional priorities.
